Divisions seize land around them while standing or moving. Capturing enemy land costs men. Hold 60% of the land to win.

Divisions never overlap and cannot be walked through. A division whose way is blocked by an enemy stops and fights until the fight ends, then carries on toward the point you ordered - for both move and formation orders, with or without Shift held.

Logistics & healing: a division draws supply along your own land to your nearest connected city. The effective distance D is measured over terrain (diagonals cost more), and a road tile costs only its terrain × 25% for logistics. Its damage output is 50% + 50%/(1 + D/20), and it reinforces from your manpower reserves at up to 3 men/s, falling with D the same way - in place, only while unengaged, so no division ever has to walk home to heal. Cut off from every city it fights at 50% output and gets no reinforcements; isolation by itself never bleeds men.

Roads: the Road tool (B) paves your own land for 2 gold per new tile, instantly, and never charges twice for the same tile. Roads make normal movement ×1.5 faster for whoever stands on them, cut logistics cost to ×25%, and survive capture along with the land.

Routing & cornered: a division wounded below 40% strength in combat routs - it flees away from the fighting toward a temporary, seeded destination 6-12 tiles off, never to a city. Uninterrupted, it recovers to 70% strength and resumes the route it had saved. If enemies intercept the rout or it is dragged back into a fight, it becomes Cornered: it can never flee again and fights to the death. Halt cancels queued orders and routes, but never a mandatory rout or cornered fight.

Push: a melee attacker that has an order shoves its defender at max(0, attacker top speed − defender top speed + 0.25) tiles/s; terrain and other bodies can stop the push. Displaced friendly holders step aside and return to exactly where they stood once the passage clears.

Columns & routes: Shift+right-click adds route checkpoints the selected divisions visit in order; a plain right-click replaces the route with a single move. Column mode (C) makes a right-click order travel single file, at least 1.2 tiles apart, in a fixed leader order that followers never overtake.

Economy: Manpower (from land, cities and farms) fills divisions; Gold (from cities, factories and land) pays for equipment and buildings. Farms can go on any of your plain land. Factories and Fortresses must stand within 5 tiles of one of your cities (select a city to see the circle). A Factory unlocks Armor and Artillery and earns gold; a Fortress makes your divisions within 3 tiles take 40% less damage. When an enemy captures a tile: a Fortress is destroyed, while a Farm or Factory changes hands intact - an unfinished building keeps its build deadline for the new owner.

Types: Infantry is balanced. Armor is fast and hits hard but costs a lot. Artillery shells enemies up to 5 tiles away but cannot capture land and is weak in melee, so escort it. Divisions far from your cities simply fight and reinforce worse (see Logistics), never losing men to distance alone. You only see enemy divisions near your divisions and cities.
